## Direction contract
THESIS: The contact form is the site's connection motif made literal: the footer's two reaching hands, pulled apart, close as the visitor answers; sending makes them touch. Refuses the heading-over-stacked-fields contact page.
OWN-WORLD: Black stage, grey Bayer-dithered hands with soft bloom, condensed uppercase title face, yellow #ffd632 only for the spark, typed answers and the send pill, mono for small labels.
STORY: The visitor sees the gap, learns four answers close it, answers one question at a time while the hands move in, and sends.
FIRST VIEWPORT: "Let's close the gap" top left, availability top right, full-bleed hands with the spark centre, question 01 with its input and the yellow Next pill at the bottom.
FORM: Close the gap (user's pick over the dealt work order). seed: none (unavailable). Signature: hands close per answer, then touch and the spark floods the screen yellow.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
